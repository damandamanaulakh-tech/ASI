# EX-014 to EX-023 — Human ↔ AI Correspondence Cases
Source: `table.csv` / mirrored as `HUMAN_AI_SEQUENCE_CORRESPONDENCE.csv`

These are correspondence examples, not claims that Human and AI mechanisms are identical.

## EX-014 — Resource Budgeting ↔ Compute/Cost Monitoring
Human side: Homeostasis, arousal, energy budgeting.
AI side: resource, latency, token and cost monitoring.
Sequence principle: Latent Result + Downstream-Critical Change.
Structural consequence: token, latency and cost states should be first-class ledger rows rather than hidden side effects.

## EX-015 — Working Memory ↔ Context Window
Human side: working memory, selective attention.
AI side: context window, attention routing, long-context.
Sequence principle: Actor View.
Structural consequence: each context window is a View; different sessions are different Sequences rather than one silently shared mind-state.

## EX-016 — Metacognition ↔ Calibration
Human side: metacognition, confidence, calibration.
AI side: uncertainty calibration and claim–evidence binding.
Sequence principle: Never Manufacture a Source.
Structural consequence: confidence must remain bound to provenance; a missing source remains a gap.

## EX-017 — Error Detection ↔ AI Repair
Human side: error detection, failure diagnosis, repair.
AI side: tool-hallucination detection and self-repair.
Sequence principle: No In-Place Loop.
Structural consequence: every repair attempt gets a new Sequence ID; the failed attempt remains historical.

## EX-018 — Theory of Mind ↔ Multi-Agent Modelling
Human side: theory of mind and perspective taking.
AI side: multi-agent modelling and audience modelling.
Sequence principle: Actor View + Seed Before Carrier.
Structural consequence: a model of another agent is a separate representation/Sequence and cannot be assumed identical to global reality or the primary actor.

## EX-019 — Moral Judgment ↔ Refusal Calibration
Human side: moral judgment versus moral action.
AI side: refusal calibration and harm-severity classification.
Sequence principle: Exception Contract.
Structural consequence: refusal or permission must identify the higher rule/contract that authorizes the exception.

## EX-020 — Social Learning ↔ Benchmark/Harness Provenance
Human side: social learning and cultural transmission.
AI side: benchmark versioning and harness provenance.
Sequence principle: Observer / Writer.
Structural consequence: training data and evaluation harnesses are recording/writer Sequences; they are not the capability being evaluated.

## EX-021 — Expertise ↔ Evaluation Boundary
Human side: expertise and boundary of competence.
AI side: evaluation-awareness and out-of-domain risk.
Sequence principle: Rule Inertia.
Structural consequence: success on an older benchmark/harness does not automatically transfer to a new scope; the rule must be re-tested.

## EX-022 — Agency ↔ Scheming/Sabotage Monitoring
Human side: agency, intent and ownership of action.
AI side: sabotage detection and scheming monitoring.
Sequence principle: Controller Mandatory.
Structural consequence: every controlled transition records who/what authorized it rather than inferring agency from the result.

## EX-023 — Memory Validation ↔ Source Conflict
Human side: memory validation and source monitoring.
AI side: source-conflict preservation and independent corroboration.
Sequence principle: Count/Quorum Threshold where the evidence contract requires it.
Structural consequence: a single-source claim can remain provisional; conflicting independent returns must not be averaged into fake consensus.
