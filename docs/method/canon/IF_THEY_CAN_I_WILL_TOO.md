## 1. THE READING

* **What people take from a success story today is the FORM.** The story is
  promoted — trading, courses, the highlight — and the watcher reads it as a
  recipe: *"i will also do the same."* They copy the visible thing.
* **Why the copy fails, and it is not weakness:** the original was **built as
  their own** — their system, their inputs, their context, their sequence.
  The visible success compresses all of that away. Re-doing "the same"
  re-does only the surface, and the surface was never the cause. *"How
  someone can re do the same"* — they cannot, structurally.
* **What actually transfers is the possibility proof.** *"If they can, i
  will too"* — take from their story only the evidence that it is possible
  for a human, then build YOUR OWN from your own ground.
* **"Failure", re-read:** under the copy stance, failure is near-guaranteed
  and then mis-read as the person's verdict. Under the possibility stance,
  their success and your path are two different sequences — the comparison
  was never valid, so its failure is not a verdict on you.

## 2. WHERE IT SITS IN HIS CANON

This is the second half of `DO_NOT_JUDGE_THE_VISIBLE_THING.md` (the
rice/MBA test). That ruling forbids **judging** the visible thing as proof
of the system behind it. This one forbids **using** the visible thing as a
template for action. Same law, both directions:

```
rice/MBA:        VISIBLE SUCCESS  -/->  "their system was X, so X works"
this teaching:   VISIBLE SUCCESS  -/->  "do X and you will succeed"
what survives:   VISIBLE SUCCESS  --->  "it is possible. Now build my own."
```

The judgment gate's hidden layers — capabilities, inputs, execution,
alternative explanations — are exactly what the copier never sees, which is
why the copy carries none of the cause.

## 3. THE STANCE, MADE MECHANICAL

`claims.py` gains `success_story_stance()`: material carrying a success
story is read for its stance —

* **TEMPLATE COPY** (*"i will also do the same"*) — never allowed to stand
  as a conclusion. It comes back carrying what was THEIRS (own-built,
  non-copyable), what TRANSFERS (the possibility), and his flip, verbatim.
* **POSSIBILITY** (*"if they can i will too"*) — his stance; kept.
* **UNSTATED** — both readings shown, neither chosen. Two readings are
  never collapsed.

Nothing is judged about the person copying — the machine refuses the
*conclusion*, not the human.
