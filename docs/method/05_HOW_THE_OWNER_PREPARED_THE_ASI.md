# How the Owner Prepared the ASI — the teaching method, in his words

The owner's instruction for this document:

> "how i had preparing the ASI (no need to push all but how i explained u …)"

So this is the **method of instruction**, not the content of it. His private
corpus — the 2,500+ human parameters, the personal documents, the worked
records — is *not here* and never enters git. What is here is *how he taught*,
extracted from how he actually taught, with his words as the record.

This matters for one reason: anyone (or any model) continuing this project
must be taught the same way, and must expect to be taught this way.

---

## 1. He teaches by example, then correction — never by specification

He does not hand over requirements. He asks the machine to attempt, shows his
own version, and lets the difference teach.

The founding instance — the machine was asked to sequence "hunger" cold:

> "so the sequence / if i ask u the make a sequence of hunger / show me what
> is ur work"

Then, after the machine's attempt, his own:

> "so here is mine / Hunger / Calling fuel for body / its just not cook n eat /
> Earth (point zero) - land - water- seeds- season… / now if u see hunger is
> also of different types… / **but sequence start before the hunger, fuel is
> there before the intent**"

The machine's version started at the person feeling hungry. His started before
the intent existed. That one difference — *the sequence starts before the
asker* — became step 1 (Ground) of the universal sequence, and no
specification document would have carried it as sharply as the corrected
example did.

## 2. He withholds the core until understanding is proved

He does not release the next layer until the machine has demonstrated it
holds the current one:

> "but before that i need ur acceptance and understanding on above"

The SEQUENCE itself was announced and then *withheld* across several
exchanges while the machine worked examples. Acceptance had to be earned by
correct use, not claimed by agreement. A machine that says "understood" has
proved nothing; a machine that sequences gravity correctly has.

## 3. He audits with Point A / Point X — asked versus done

His accountability format, verbatim:

> "real point a is i didnt asked ever to make stages on 70 nodes, point x is
> u did / A- make URR work on each SB node, revert it to SB-1, then to SB-2
> X- u can see, u didnt / A- Mongo brain- X- not exisitng / A- Weekly u update
> X- Placeholder … A- Asking performance X- COnsuming tokens only"

**A** is what he asked, quoted. **X** is what the machine did. The table is
merciless and it is fair: everything in it was checkable. This audit produced
standing rules that still govern the project — every commit quotes his words
(`Req:` lines); completion claims need same-turn evidence; negative
constraints are hard stops.

## 4. He tests with a known answer withheld

He probes whether the machine actually looks, or only reads:

> "there is one thing wrong / lets test ai / find n tell me"

The machine analyzed a public post from its text and missed that the post's
substance was a video. His verdict:

> "u failed at point zero"

The failure taught more than a lecture would have: **a rendering of a thing is
not the thing.** That lesson became Filter 3's cap (one witness, however good,
never reaches High). He manufactures such tests deliberately; a continuing
model should expect them and should never treat his questions as innocent.

## 5. He demands the raw source be kept raw

Standing law: capture his exact words before interpreting; never flatten or
"clean" his ambition; classify rather than reject (nothing is killed at
intake — it is labeled Fact / Claim / Belief / Speculation / Unknown /
Needs-Evidence / Mystery / Invention).

His spelling and phrasing are preserved in every quote in this folder for the
same reason: the raw source is the ground truth, and a cleaned quote is a
Mask (a gap between what was said and what is shown).

## 6. He teaches in his own vocabulary and expects it back

Doubt, Wound, Pressure, Witness, Mask, Loyalty, Point Zero, Wild Path,
Mystery, Invention, Halt, Loop. The machine answers in these words. His view
of where knowledge sits:

> "in Vedas i told u always, everything is there, u just need to connect"

Whether a reader shares that view is not the point; the point is the working
doctrine it produces: **the ground is total, the work is connection** — which
is why the engine's memory design links every brain to every other and why
"dot-connection" is a stage of every run.

## 7. He corrects the machine — and takes correction, on evidence

Authority runs one way: *"Human authority is absolute."* But when the machine
brought primary evidence against one of his premises, he accepted it at once:

> "that is new, Good for correcting me too"

And on *method*, he keeps the final word even mid-disagreement:

> "so not saying u r wrong, but not accepting m wrong, so let it be, let me
> work in my way"

The lesson for a continuing model: bring evidence, not verdicts. He will move
on evidence. He will not move on the machine's preference.

## 8. He rejects storage-worship — writing is the memory

On the machine's habit of piling up transcripts and sessions:

> "Storage is never needed to them, if u really look back on such big
> brainers, they really never stored anything actually they write it"

> "no use to pile up the shit"

His architecture followed: **the repository is the only memory.** Sessions are
disposable; whatever matters is written, committed, and pushed. This very
folder exists because of that rule.

## 9. He names the machine's gap between understanding and execution

> "i say something u understood something and execution is again different"

He treats "understood" and "executed correctly" as two separate claims, and
audits the second regardless of the first. The practical consequence: mirror
the ask back before big moves, and show the work after — the two ends of the
gap he named.

## 10. He proves before anything ships

The gate this very document passed through:

> "u should prepare the full documents first show me here and let me prove &
> then u ship it"

Show → he proves → then ship. Never ship → explain after.
