# 12a · WHAT CHANGED

### Every new thing, every tweak, every shift in meaning the walk produced

**What this article is.** A ledger of change. Not a summary of the Riemann Hypothesis —
an account of what is *different* after the walk than before it, item by item, with the
evidence attached and the failures kept in.

**Tags:** `[FACT]` published or provable · `[ARCHIVE]` read from his manuscripts ·
`[MEASURED]` computed here, re-runnable · `[READING]` defensible interpretation, can be
overturned · `[STRUCK]` claimed, then withdrawn.

**Nothing in this article proves anything about the Riemann Hypothesis, and nothing in it
tries to.**

---

# PART ONE · THE CHANGES IN MEANING

These are the shifts where a sentence everyone already had came to mean something
different.

## 1 · The hypothesis is a statement about one object's NATURE, not about infinitely many locations

**Before:** "prove that all the non-trivial zeros lie on the critical line." A claim about
where infinitely many things are.

**After:** his own sentence is ***die Wurzeln sind reell*** — *the roots are real.* A claim
about **what one function is like.** `[ARCHIVE — verified in four of his documents]`

**Why the difference is not cosmetic.** A totality over infinity — *every* zero, forever —
cannot be reached by counting. No finite instrument gets there; the walk's own census is
the demonstration of that, not an exception to it. **Totalities are never counted into
truth; they are forced by structure.** So a proof cannot be *about the zeros* at all. It
must be about an **object whose nature makes an off-line zero impossible.**

This one re-reading determines the whole shape of Part Three. It is the single largest
change the walk produced, and it came from reading his words rather than the world's
paraphrase of them.

## 2 · The ½ is his coordinate's centre of symmetry — not his claim

`[ARCHIVE]` Riemann wrote in a coordinate where the critical line sits at the origin of
symmetry. **The "½" everyone quotes is a feature of the frame he chose**, not the content
of what he asserted. What he asserted is reality of roots.

**Consequence:** arguments that treat ½ as a mysterious constant to be explained are
explaining an artefact of notation. The mystery is *realness*, not *one-half*.

## 3 · If it is true, it is true by nothing to spare

`[FACT — Rodgers–Tao 2018]` The de Bruijn–Newman constant satisfies **Λ ≥ 0**, and
RH ⟺ Λ ≤ 0.

**Therefore: if RH is true, Λ = 0 exactly.**

**The change in meaning:** the hypothesis does not sit comfortably inside a region of
truth with margin around it. **It sits precisely on the boundary between two regimes.**
Any true proof has to explain not just why it holds, but why it holds *by exactly
nothing*. "Barely true" is not a figure of speech here; it is the measured position.

## 4 · Off-centre things exist only in pairs, and the pair never finishes forming

`[FACT]` The functional equation ξ(s) = ξ(1−s) makes the strip symmetric about its middle.
A zero off the middle **cannot be alone** — the mirror law forces a twin on the other
side. A zero *at* the middle is its own reflection.

`[READING]` Stated freshly, and this is the walk's own sentence:

> **Every truth of this function stands at the middle — the only place where a thing is
> its own mirror, and the only place where its imaginary part can be rotated away. Nothing
> steps off-centre, because off-centre things must come in pairs, and the pair never
> finishes forming.**

## 5 · "Provisionally left aside" — he declared his own halt inside his own paper

`[ARCHIVE]` ***"vorläufig bei Seite gelassen."***

**Before:** read as an apology, or as evidence he could not do it.

**After:** it is a *method statement*. He did not fail to prove it and cover that over. He
**named the setting-aside, in the published paper, with a reason.** The strongest single
piece of method in the man, and it changes how the eight pages should be read: not as an
incomplete argument, but as a complete announcement with a declared open item inside it.

## 6 · His count holds; his mechanism does not

`[MEASURED]` His first named debt — the claim about how many roots the strip owes below a
height — was reconstructed and measured at the points where his heuristic should have been
strongest.

**The count is right. The reasoning he sketched for it does not survive measurement.**

**Change in meaning:** a correct result reached by a route that does not hold is not a
lucky guess; it is a signal that the true route is something else, and that the something
else is still missing. The census then forced a further correction to the walk's own first
reading of that halt — recorded in place rather than smoothed away.

# PART TWO · THE NEW THINGS BUILT

## 7 · A census of 63,519 zeros, with an exact ledger

`[MEASURED]`

| | |
|---|---|
| Reach | height **50,000.4** |
| Zeros held | **63,519** |
| Independent count (`mpmath.nzeros`) | **63,519** |
| **Deficit** | **0** |
| Arches (consecutive pairs) | 63,515 |

Banked block by block, 1,000 height each, every block's own count checked against
prediction at landing. **The census doubled from an earlier 27,000 reach and the ledger
stayed exact through the doubling.** Anyone with the files can recount it.

**What it is worth, stated plainly:** it is an instrument, and instruments are not
theorems. 63,519 zeros checked is 63,519 zeros checked. **It says nothing about the
63,520th.**

## 8 · A measured bound on the correction-to-the-correction

`[MEASURED]` The second walk registered its test **before any of its data existed** — the
null model named, the estimator frozen, three verdicts fixed in advance, and an honest
power note written down.

**Result: every height bin |z| < 2, pooled z = −0.75. The registered verdict fired as
BBLM CONFIRMED-DEEPER.**

**And immediately, the limit on it**, because the registration demanded it in advance:
this is **a bound, not proof of zero residual.** The power note predicted ~83 expected
counts; the measurement landed at 83.83, so the estimate was accurate. **What is
established: no correction-to-the-correction larger than roughly a quarter of the known
correction's own size exists at these heights.** Anything smaller is invisible to this
instrument.

## 9 · One thing carried forward without being promoted

`[MEASURED]` The residual is **negative at all three cuts**, and grows more negative as
the cut tightens: **−0.75, −0.65, −2.17.**

At the tightest cut it would have read *ambiguous* rather than confirmed. **The primary cut
was named in advance and it is not that one.** Choosing the most interesting secondary
after seeing the data is precisely the error Part Four is about.

**It is recorded because it is real and the next walk should watch it. It is not a
finding.**

## 10 · A new census minimum — reported, not headlined

`[MEASURED]` **δ = 0.040746289184 at γ = 36,510.1664**, below the previous record.

The treatment was fixed in advance: *a new record is reported, not headlined — the null
owns small gaps now.* Under the null, a census of this size is **expected** to throw a
minimum near this value. **It is the instrument working, not a discovery.**

## 11 · The drum's gap has a name

`[MEASURED]` + `[READING]` Six circuits of the conjectured operator whose spectrum would
be the zeros. The finding:

> **Every drum ever built secures two of three corners and pays the third.**

| corner | requirement |
|---|---|
| **law** | the right zero count |
| **guard** | the right statistics |
| **mirror** | self-adjointness |

**No mechanism holds law + guard + mirror at once, unconditionally.** The walk also
measured what any true drum must contain — the return comb with periods r·ln p and weights
1/(r²pʳ) — and followed it twice.

**This is a real gap, not a misread.** Naming it is the contribution; closing it is not
claimed.

## 12 · The driver is the smallest it can be and still rule

`[MEASURED]` The explicit formula, run: the visible staircase of primes is driven by an
invisible sum over the zeros. Zeros on the half-line make the error term **√x**.

**The change in meaning:** ½ is not an arbitrary place. It is **the position at which the
hidden driver is as small as it can possibly be while still governing the whole visible
count.** Any other line makes the driver larger. The hypothesis is, read this way, a claim
that the world is run by the smallest possible hidden term.

# PART THREE · THE FOUR SHAPES A PROOF COULD TAKE

`[SYNTHETIC — enumeration argued, not proved]` The reverse walk starts at the finished
sentence and descends: *if the proven state exists, what can its last line be?*

| Shape | The last line reads | What must stand under it |
|---|---|---|
| **A — the self-mirrored source** | the zeros are the spectrum of a self-adjoint thing; self-adjoint spectra are real | a drum with **all three** corners, from one mechanism, unconditionally |
| **B — the impossible intruder** | assume one zero off the middle; then Q > 0 and Q ≤ 0 | a quantity Q every off-middle zero forces positive and the ground forces non-positive |
| **C — the nature of ξ** | ξ belongs to the real-rooted class; membership survives the limit | ξ a limit of real-rooted structures **and** real-rootedness not lost at the boundary |
| **D — transfer** | statement X is proved, and X ⟺ RH | an X whose own proof is A, B or C — **transfer alone never closes** |

**D collapses into the others. The true fork is A / B / C.** Each descent meets a named
gap: A meets the three-corner triangle; B meets positivity; C meets boundary survival.

**And this is where Part One §1 pays off:** none of the three shapes is a count. **A census
cannot be a last line**, because a last line about a totality must be structural. The
instrument built in §7 was never a candidate proof and was never presented as one.

# PART FOUR · WHAT WAS GOT WRONG, AND WHAT IT COST

The most useful changes came from failures. They are kept.

## 13 · A finding was made, hardened, and then dissolved

`[STRUCK]` The first walk measured a small-gap suppression, watched it survive a
pre-registered threshold, and hardened it.

**Then the literature gate ran. It was already known** — a documented finite-height
correction. Measured match 0.63 against the published 0.65; the p-value re-priced from
0.075 to 0.187.

**The finding is struck and stays struck**, with its dissolution recorded beside it.

**The method lesson, bought with the finding:**

> ### A pre-registration must name its NULL.

A test registered against *nothing* will find something. Registering a threshold is not
enough; you must state **what the world would look like if the effect were absent** — and
the honest version of that is usually the field's existing best model, not zero.

**The second walk named its null. The null held.** That is a smaller result and a sounder
one.

## 14 · One of my own conclusions was wrong, and the file keeps the title

`[STRUCK]` The "lost expansion" was reported lost. **It is not lost** — it is the
Riemann–Siegel formula, found in his papers by a librarian before 1900 and published in
1932. The document recording this is titled *"the expansion is not lost, and one of my
conclusions was wrong."*

**The title was not changed after the fact.** A record that only shows the corrected state
teaches nothing about how the correction happened.

# PART FIVE · WHAT THE MANUSCRIPTS CHANGED

`[ARCHIVE]` Read directly, folio by folio, with per-line confidence marks and unreadable
words written `⟨…⟩` rather than guessed.

## 15 · The provenance of the 1859 paper

**The eight pages went out as an enclosure to a letter returning Gauss's letter to Encke** —
the letter in which Gauss describes counting primes by hand and using the logarithmic
integral. **That material was unpublished until 1863. Riemann had it in his hands, in
copy, in the weeks he wrote.**

**Change in meaning:** the paper is usually read as arriving from nowhere. It arrives
**with a specific unpublished document open on the desk.**

## 16 · There is a documented physical step where a misreading could enter

**Riemann's draft → another man's copy → the 1876 printed edition → the world's version.**

`[READING]` This does not show that anything *was* misread. It shows the "misread"
hypothesis has a real physical mechanism rather than being a suspicion — and it locates
where to look.

## 17 · What the leaves hold

| Source | What it changed |
|---|---|
| Folios 19r–20r, transcribed line by line | the working draft behind the eight pages |
| The same folios, struck text recovered on a **second** pass | the first pass reported "letterforms visible underneath" and stopped — that was not good enough |
| Leaves 21, 26 | **the hypothesis asserted flatly**, in his hand |
| Leaf 22 | **his own French fair copy** — where he deleted his own thank-you paragraph |
| Leaves 23r–24v | **the cut part of the draft letter, recovered** — five never-printed passages |

## 18 · He left an instruction nobody executed

`[ARCHIVE]` On leaf 26r: ***"Es ist mir daran gelegen, daß meine Formel auch praktischen
Rechnern bekannt würde"*** — *it matters to me that my formula should also become known to
practical computers.*

And a directive of his own that was never carried out as he stated it: **track the
influence of the individual periodic terms.**

**Change in meaning:** he wanted the thing *computed*, by working calculators, not only
proved. **The census in §7 is closer to what he asked for than any proof attempt is** —
and that is a fact about his intent, not a justification of the census.

# PART SIX · THE METHOD CHANGES THE WALK PRODUCED

Findings about *how to work*, which outlast the subject.

| # | The change |
|---|---|
| 19 | **A pre-registration must name its null.** §13. Cost: one struck finding. |
| 20 | **Report a record, do not headline it.** When the null already predicts a value, observing it is the instrument working. |
| 21 | **Check the ledger before the result.** A bad count voids everything downstream, so the count is verified first, every time. |
| 22 | **Two witnesses that differ halt.** They are never averaged — an average of two conflicting measurements is a number neither supports, wearing false precision. |
| 23 | **Reverse must be paired with forward.** A descent always terminates and always produces a story; nothing inside it can tell you the story is right. **The forward return on this walk has not been run** — named as owed. |

# WHAT THIS ARTICLE DOES NOT CLAIM

- **No proof, no partial proof, no path claimed to be the path.**
- **No claim about any zero other than the 63,519 checked.**
- **No claim that the verdict in §8 says anything about the hypothesis.** It measures gap
  statistics among zeros *already known* to be on the line.
- **No claim of priority or novelty for anything in Part Two.** No literature search was
  run against the whole field, and §13 is what happens when that step is skipped.

**The one sentence, if only one is read:**

> The largest change is not a result. It is that the hypothesis stopped being a claim
> about where infinitely many things are and became a claim about what one thing is — and
> that reframing is what makes a census an instrument rather than a candidate proof.
