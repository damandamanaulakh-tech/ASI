# 3 · THE INSTRUMENT THAT DECIDES — the trace formula

This is the hinge of the whole article, and it is standard mathematics, not interpretation.

**In quantum chaos**, the Gutzwiller trace formula says: the **density of energy levels**
of a quantum system equals a smooth term plus **a sum over the closed orbits of the
corresponding classical system**, each orbit contributing an oscillation whose frequency
is that orbit's **period**. `[FACT]`

> **spectrum ← sum over periodic orbits**

**In Riemann's world**, the explicit formula says: the **counting of primes** equals a
smooth term plus **a sum over the zeros**, each zero contributing an oscillation whose
frequency is γ. Read the other way, the **density of zeros** is given by a sum over the
primes, with **log p** appearing exactly where a period appears. `[FACT]`

> **zeros ← sum over primes, with periods log p**

**These are the same shape.** Not analogy — the same functional form, which is why the
Hilbert–Pólya program exists at all and why Berry and Keating proposed that the missing
operator is the quantisation of **xp**, whose classical orbits have periods log p.
`[FACT — a published, live research programme; not settled]`

**And our own walk measured this object.** The return comb: **periods r·log p, weights
1/(r²pʳ)**, followed twice. `[MEASURED]`
