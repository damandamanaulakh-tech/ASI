## 2 · What his own Nachlass says the MAIN work was [READING]

The famous fragment, printed with his collected works, verbatim:

> *"Meine Hauptarbeit betrifft eine neue Auffassung der bekannten
> Naturgesetze — Ausdruck derselben mittelst anderer Grundbegriffe —
> wodurch die Benutzung der experimentellen Data über die Wechselwirkung
> zwischen Wärme, Licht, Magnetismus und Electricität zur Erforschung
> ihres **Zusammenhangs** möglich wurde. Ich wurde dazu hauptsächlich durch
> das Studium der Werke Newton's, Euler's und — anderseits — Herbart's
> geführt."*

*My MAIN WORK concerns a new conception of the known laws of nature — their
expression through different fundamental concepts — making it possible to
use the experimental data on the interplay of heat, light, magnetism and
electricity to explore their CONNECTION. I was led to this chiefly by
studying Newton, Euler and — on the other side — Herbart.*

Around that fragment stands the rest of the record [READING]:

- In the philosophical fragments he calls himself a **Herbartian in
  psychology and epistemology** (while declining Herbart's natural
  philosophy) — the mind-model he carried was Herbart's **Geistesmassen**:
  thoughts as formed masses that enter, bind, persist and pull on one
  another.
- The 1854 habilitation lecture closes by placing the ground of space's
  metric **outside geometry, in binding forces** — geometry as the study
  of how the continuum is held together. The road that became general
  relativity.
- 1858: his electrodynamics paper — electric action **propagating** at
  light speed; force as flow through the medium, not action at a distance.
- He died in 1866 at thirty-nine. The Hauptarbeit stayed fragments. The
  supplement stayed a promise.
