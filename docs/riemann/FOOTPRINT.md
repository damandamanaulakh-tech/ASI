# THE FOOTPRINT — map of the RH walk

One page that answers three questions at any time: **where did each loop open ·
what did we get there · where are we now.** It is written to be read alone —
every mark defined, every station carrying its yield.

**Legend** · ● closed (finding banked) · ○ open (loop standing, needs a move)
· ✕ closed **against us** (the hoped reading died — that is a result, not a
failure) · ⟲ correction (the path bent back on itself; recorded, never
erased) · **[his]** = a station opened by the owner's explicit decision,
quoted verbatim.

**Point Zero** (the owner's original ask, exact words):

> "down the line objective is, does the RH what we are proving is same what
> Reimann said or its tweaked / once we confirmed we will step ahead / second
> is we are matching his own words and patterns of meaning so we take it in
> right direction"

## 3 · The corrections record (the walk's honesty)

Three times the path bent back on itself; each is kept visible in the archive
rather than rewritten, because a map that hides its wrong turns is a Mask:

| ⟲ | Wrong claim | Killed by | What replaced it |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | "the paper merely hedges" | reading the full sentence | he printed that his attempts *failed* — the field's framing is his own |
| B | "his named debts were paid (von Mangoldt)" | one word — *reelle* | N₀(T) is open; it became the new Point Zero |
| C | "the instrument provably cannot produce the proof — his road ends" | our own census | count exact to T=1000; only localization dies |

Correction B is the hinge of the whole walk: an error, caught, that *pointed*
— the reversed reading exposed the one debt still standing, and the owner made
it the new Point Zero.

## 4 · What left this trail and became something else

The method used on this walk — two witnesses, the Mask, halts named not
patched, loops at every halt — was extracted, written as seven documents,
proved by the owner, and shipped into the engine as **the seven filters**.
The RH walk is therefore also the engine's origin story: the archive stays
here; the method lives in `docs/method/` and in `src/sourceborn/filters.py`.
