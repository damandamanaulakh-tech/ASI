# PART 1 · POINT ZERO — the middle

His frame, preserved as he spoke it:

> *"there is no start. There is no end. This is always… you are always in a middle. When
> you are in a middle, you are always in an on a half sides."*

## The fact layer `[FACT]`

1. The strip where the zeros live runs from 0 to 1. Riemann's ξ obeys the mirror law
   **ξ(s) = ξ(1−s)** — the strip is symmetric about its middle, and the middle is **½**.
2. **A zero off the middle cannot be alone.** The mirror law forces a twin on the other
   side. Off-middle zeros exist only as mirrored pairs; a zero *at* the middle is its own
   reflection.
3. On the middle — and only there — the complex function rotates into a **real wave**:
   Z(t) = e^{iθ(t)}ζ(½+it) is real, and θ(t) is exactly known. *On the half, the
   imaginary can be turned away; off the half, no rotation makes the thing real.*
4. **Riemann's own sentence** (verified in four of his documents): *the roots are real.*
   The ½ is his coordinate's centre of symmetry, **not his claim**. `[ARCHIVE]`
5. The zeros never end (Hardy 1914). **No finite check can close this** — structurally,
   any computed height is a middle, never an end.
6. **Λ ≥ 0** (Rodgers–Tao 2018), and RH ⟺ Λ ≤ 0. **So if RH is true, Λ = 0 exactly: the
   truth sits on the boundary between regimes — "barely true."**

## The statement, fresh `[READING]`

> Every truth of this function stands at the middle — the only place where a thing is its
> own mirror, and the only place where its imaginary part can be rotated away. Nothing
> steps off-centre, because off-centre things must come in pairs, and the pair never
> finishes forming.

**Point 4 is the load-bearing correction of the whole project.** The world says "prove
where the zeros are." **Riemann's sentence says what ξ IS** — *die Wurzeln sind reell*,
the roots of a specific function are real. A statement about **one object's nature**, not
about infinitely many locations. Totalities are never counted into truth; they are forced
by structure.

# PART 2 · THE MAN

Files: `HIS_WORDS_TRANSLATED.md` · `THE_USE.md` · `THE_UNFINISHED.md` ·
`HERBART_AND_LETTERS_LEDGER.md`

## How he worked `[ARCHIVE]`

- **He named his own halt inside the work.** The 1859 paper's most important sentence for
  method is *"vorläufig bei Seite gelassen"* — **provisionally left aside.** He did not
  fail to prove it and hide that; he declared the setting-aside, in the paper, with a
  reason. **The single strongest piece of method in the man**, and the engine only half
  has it.
- **Geistesmassen** — his own doctrine of mind-masses: thoughts as bodies that attract,
  combine and persist. He had a theory of thinking before he had this theory of numbers,
  and it came from Herbart. `HERBART_AND_LETTERS_LEDGER.md` traces it.
- **He wrote almost nothing down.** The 1859 paper is eight pages. The working was in
  the Nachlass and much of it was burned by his housekeeper after his death.
- **He died at 39**, of tuberculosis, under a fig tree at Selasca, mid-sentence in his
  work.

## What he wanted the theory FOR `[READING]`, from `THE_USE.md`

Not the primes for their own sake. The evidence in his own Nachlass points at a **main
work** (*Hauptarbeit*) about the laws of nature — a unified account in which the number
theory was an instrument. His own fragment: a *"new conception of the known laws of
nature."*

**The owner's reading, which the walk confirmed:** the gap is not in the mathematics.
**The gap is what he wanted to do with it, and he never said.**

## His bucket `[ARCHIVE]`, from `THE_UNFINISHED.md`

Inventoried into four classes: **unfinished** (started, never completed) · **left alone**
(carried far, then withheld by his own hand) · **not linked** (joints his programme
implies that his ink never made) · **the live remainder** (still nobody's).

## WAY 1 — THE GUARD `WAY1_THE_GUARD_MEASURED.md` (526 lines)

*How close does the world come to the line, and does anything guard it?*

- **First walk** (§11–§13): a small-gap suppression measured at 26,000 and hardened at
  27,000 — then **the literature gate dissolved it.** It was BBLM's already-known
  finite-height correction (arXiv math/0602270). Match 0.63 against their 0.65; the
  p-value re-priced from 0.075 to 0.187.
- **The method lesson, which cost the finding:** ***a pre-registration must name its
  NULL.*** A test registered against "nothing" will find something.
- **Second walk** (§14–§15): registered **before any data existed above 27,000**, with
  BBLM as the null, a frozen estimator, three verdicts fixed in advance, and an honest
  power note. **Landed 2026-08-05 at height 50,000.**

> **REGISTERED VERDICT: BBLM CONFIRMED-DEEPER.** Every height bin |z| < 2; pooled
> z = −0.75 on 77 observed against 83.83 expected. `[MEASURED]`

**And its limit, stated in the same breath:** a **bound, not proof of zero residual.**
The registration's own power note said a next-order term below ~25% of BBLM's correction
stays invisible at this reach — and the predicted expected count (~83) landed at 83.83,
so the power estimate was accurate.

**Carried forward without promotion:** the residual is negative at all three cuts and
grows more negative as the cut tightens (−0.75, −0.65, **−2.17** at δ<0.08). At the
tightest cut it would have read AMBIGUOUS. The primary cut was named in advance as 0.12,
and choosing the most interesting secondary afterwards is exactly the §13 failure.

`REVERSE_WALK.md`. **His order:** *"On this u cant even do reverse engineering, can u do
that / then lets do."*

Forward walking (167 years of it) starts at the ground and tries to reach the sentence.
**This walk starts AT the sentence** — assumes the proven state exists — and descends.

**Level −1: the only possible last steps** `[SYNTHETIC]` (enumeration argued, not proved):

| shape | the last line reads | what must stand under it |
|---|---|---|
| **A — the self-mirrored source** | the zeros are the spectrum of a self-adjoint thing; self-adjoint spectra are real | the drum with **all three** corners from one mechanism, unconditionally |
| **B — the impossible intruder** | assume one zero off the middle; then Q > 0 and Q ≤ 0 | a quantity Q every off-middle zero forces positive and the ground forces non-positive |
| **C — the nature of ξ** | ξ belongs to the real-rooted class; membership survives the limit | ξ a limit of real-rooted structures **and** real-rootedness not lost at the boundary |
| **D — transfer** | X is proved, and X ⟺ RH | an equivalent X whose own proof is A, B or C — **transfer alone never closes** |

**D collapses into the others. The true fork is A / B / C.** Descending each meets a
named gap: A meets the three-corner triangle; B meets Weil positivity; C meets the
boundary-survival problem.

**And the correction this project owes itself:** his method is **reverse AND forward** —
*Artifact Reverse-Engineering and Deduction* — two chains that must meet. A descent alone
always terminates and always produces a story, and nothing inside it can tell you the
story is right. **The forward return on the RH reverse walk has not been run.** Named as
owed.

# PART 8 · THE FIVE THINGS WORTH SAYING PLAINLY

1. **Riemann's sentence is about an object's nature, not a location.** Everything
   downstream changes once that is taken seriously, including what a proof would have to
   look like — shape A, B or C, never a count.
2. **No finite verification can ever close this**, and the project's own census is the
   proof of that rather than an exception to it.
3. **The drum's gap has a name.** law + guard + mirror, never all three at once,
   unconditionally.
4. **A pre-registration must name its null.** Bought with a finding that had to be struck.
5. **He declared his own halt inside his own paper.** *Vorläufig bei Seite gelassen.*
   That is the method, from the man, and it outranks everything the walk measured.
