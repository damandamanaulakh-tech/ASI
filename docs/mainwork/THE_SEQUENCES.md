## 1 · THE UNIVERSAL SEQUENCE — the spine

**Status: WORKING, NOT FINAL.** His own words: *"there is one universal sequence for
everything / and we have to define that"* and later *"the universal sequence, which
is yet to built."* The eight steps below are what the work runs on today; he has not
closed them.

| 2 | **PRESSURE** | Something forces contact with it. |

| 4 | **WITNESS** | Someone stops *using* it and *looks* at it. Rare, and a choice. |
| 5 | **EXPRESSION** | A way to *say* it is found. This — not step 1 — is the human act. |
| 6 | **NAMING** | The name begins to stand for the thing. **This is where the Mask forms.** |
| 7 | **HALT** | The expression fails somewhere. There is always a somewhere. |
| 8 | **LOOP** | The halt becomes the next GROUND. |

`[HIS WORDS]` on step 8: *"U need always a end point to verify, but that point is a
point zero for new loop, that is halt, that is invention."*

`[HIS WORDS]` on the order: *"yeah / u can see the sequence, every where things have
there own sequence / but there is one universal sequence for everything / and we
have to define that / all parameters and nodes, everything is for that only."*

**Load-bearing detail:** USE (3) comes BEFORE WITNESS (4). Witness is defined as the
moment someone *stops using* it and looks — so use must already be running. This was
got wrong once in the archetype test and struck by him.

**The one sanctioned exception, his rule:** an INVENTION has no ground to find and
enters at step 5 (Expression). *"cricket and all Is inventions… consider it as
