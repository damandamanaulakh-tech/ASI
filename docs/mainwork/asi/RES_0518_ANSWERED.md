## The bigger finding — why all 650 are held

**The reason is identical on every one of the 650 rows**, word for word:

> *"Present in the fully enumerated v1.0 list but outside the earlier v0.2 target count
> of 36 for this container."*

**They were not held for quality. They were held for arithmetic.** A target count was
fixed first, the full enumeration overflowed it, and the overflow was parked.

**Three consequences, stated plainly:**

1. **The active 2,560 is a number, not a judgement.** Which parameters are "in" was
   decided by where a container's target ran out, not by which parameters matter. Nothing
   was lost — he kept every one, deleted nothing — but the boundary between active and
   reserve carries no information about importance.
2. **It explains the 24–48 spread.** Different containers had different targets, so
   different amounts overflowed. The uneven pyramid is a residue of the count-first
   build.
3. **It is the same pattern as the architecture versions.** 2,000 → 2,560 → 2,592 →
   3,072: each step is a target being raised and reserve being promoted. **The
   architecture question and the reserve question are one question.**
