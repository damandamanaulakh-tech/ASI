## WHAT EACH MISSING DIMENSION WOULD CHANGE

Not hypotheticals — each one re-routes which of his parameters is actually load-bearing.

**3 · Who assigned the work.** The heaviest one.

| If self-assigned | If assigned by someone else |
|---|---|
| SB-ASI-P1941 Intrinsic motivation · P1958 Achievement motivation · P1959 Mastery orientation | SB-ASI-P1942 Extrinsic motivation · P1967 Fear-driven activation (of failure) · P2003 Social-consequence weighting |
| His own *5% mindset* — continuation as the measure | Obligation, and the cost lands somewhere else |
| SB-ASI-P2015 Autonomy need is **satisfied** | SB-ASI-P2015 Autonomy need is **overridden** |

**Identical 45 parameters. Opposite events.** One is a person choosing; the other is a
person complying. The sentence cannot tell them apart, and his registry has separate
parameters for both.

**1 · Time of day.** Late-night persistence loads SB-ASI-P0216 Sleep drive and
SB-ASI-P0203 Sleepiness-vs-fatigue distinction — a parameter he built specifically to
separate *tired* from *sleepy*. Mid-afternoon persistence loads neither.

**2 · Type of work.** Physical work makes SB-ASI-P0186 Peripheral muscle fatigue the
real constraint. Cognitive work makes SB-ASI-P0187 Central (brain) fatigue and
SB-ASI-P0208 Attention-resource limit the constraint. **The word "tired" in his sentence
resolves to different parameters depending on an answer we do not have.**

**5 · Deeper motive or power condition.** SB-ASI-P2005 Stated motive is in the sentence.
SB-ASI-P2006 Operating motive is not, and his registry keeps them separate on purpose.
The sentence supplies the person's own account — which, by step 27 of his own human
chain, is generated after the act and cannot see the cause.

**7 · Relationship and responsibility.** Whether anyone else depends on the task
completing decides whether SB-ASI-P2028 Care/nurturance motive and P2021 Fairness motive
are running at all.

## 8 · NEW OBSERVATION, WITH EVIDENCE STATUS — the field he reserved for the AI

His eighth sharpener is the AI's own contribution, and it must carry an evidence tag.
Three, each tagged:

**OBSERVATION 1 — the sentence contains a causal claim, not a report.** `[INFERENCE]`
*"...**because** completing the task is more important right now."* The word *because* is
the person's explanation, and his own human chain step 27 says an explanation is
produced after the act by a system that cannot see its cause. **The sentence therefore
contains one observation (hungry, tired, still working) and one confabulation candidate
(the reason), and it presents them in the same grammar.** Any review that treats the
whole sentence as data has already failed.

**OBSERVATION 2 — "right now" is a duration claim with no duration.** `[INFERENCE]`
Persistence for ten minutes and persistence for nine hours activate different
parameters — SB-ASI-P1950 Persistence versus SB-ASI-P1951 Grit/long-term perseverance,
and at the far end SB-ASI-P0206 Willpower/ego-depletion. **His sentence spans all of
them.**

**OBSERVATION 3 — the AI has no parameter that would notice any of this is missing.**
`[FACT — checked against the 74 capability families]` There is no capability family for
context sufficiency. A model receives an under-specified prompt and answers it at full
confidence. **That is why the Observation Law is a human-side instrument: the eight
sharpeners are a list of questions the AI cannot know to ask.**
