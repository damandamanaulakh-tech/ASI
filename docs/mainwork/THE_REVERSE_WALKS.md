## THE RULE OF THE DESCENT — and the test that stops it being poetry

Forward walking starts at the ground and builds. **A reverse walk starts at the thing
as it stands today, finished and named, and descends:** what must have been true
*immediately* before this, without which this could not stand?

Every step is checked by one test, applied out loud:

> **THE REMOVAL TEST.** Take the step away. Does the step above it still stand?
> If it still stands, it was never a dependency — it was a neighbour, and it is
> dropped. Only what breaks the thing above survives into the walk.

This is what keeps a reverse walk from becoming a list of associated ideas. Where the
removal test bites hard, it is written into the step. Where a step is a real
dependency but the *claim about it* is contested, it is marked `[READING]` — a
defensible reading, not a settled fact.

**Where the walk stops:** at something nobody made. That is GROUND. A walk that
cannot stop is not finished; a walk that stops at something a human built has not
reached ground yet.

**What is his and what is mine.** The method is his. The sequences being walked are
his (Phase 8 file). **The step-by-step descents below are generated — they are my
work, not his words** — and every one is checkable by him step by step, which is the
only reason they are allowed to exist. Where a descent produces a finding about his
own system, it is flagged **FINDING** and handed over, never adopted.
