## Overclaim risk

The strongest risk is confusing **symbol-language generation** with **historical decipherment**.

Safe wording:

> “This is an ARD symbolic reading using the image as a reference matrix, not a verified translation.”

Unsafe wording:

> “This is what the Egyptians meant.”

## Promotion requirements

To promote any synthetic claim, we need:

1. Higher-resolution image.
2. Provenance/object source.
3. Exact sign inventory.
4. Reading direction.
5. Transliteration.
6. Translation by reliable Egyptology source.
7. Comparison with known royal names/formulae.
8. Identification of damaged/missing signs.
