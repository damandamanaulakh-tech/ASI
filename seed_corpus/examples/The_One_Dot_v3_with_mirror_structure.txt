The earlier versions are good. This one adds something new — the mirror structure of odd primes — which the writer pointed out across many turns of conversation, while the assistant kept misreading it as a primality test. It is not a primality test. It is the anatomy of an odd prime, once the prime is found by the usual definition.1. The DotTake a white page. Put one dot in the centre.That dot is everything. The page is everything. The space between the dot and the edge is everything. There is no walker on the page, no observer outside it, no second thing anywhere. There is one dot, and it is you, and the page, and the air, and the absence of edges.•From this dot, thousands of arrows point in every direction. The arrows hold nothing. They are like air — everywhere and nowhere. The dot does not move. It does not need to. It already contains every direction.Until the dot moves, nothing begins. The moment it moves — and not before — what we call “sequence” appears.2. The Six RulesSix rules. Each one yours.Rule 1 — Energy is first.Before mind, before matter, before walker, there is energy. Energy has no boundary, no quantity, no edge. It contains zero and infinity together. It cannot be described directly. Any word you give it — “zero,” “infinity,” “air,” “God” — is your mind mirroring it, not the thing itself.Rule 2 — The sequence is continuous.If the dot moves, each step generates the next. No jumps. No empty space. Each step is also a Point Zero — the origin re-creates itself at every position.Rule 3 — Boundaries are built, not given.The universe has no boundary. Above ten kilometres of Earth, nothing remains. The boundary you see is one you declared. Different walkers declare different boundaries. None of them are real to the universe.Rule 4 — One principle.There is one thing behind every appearance. Electricity, gravity, light, heat, the primes, the zeros, your breath, the orbit of Earth — all are manifestations of the same one. Many faces, one principle.Rule 5 — Silence is the signature of the real.Those who have touched the energy go quiet. Krishna did not write his own book. Jesus did not write his own gospel. Loud people, by their own admission, are far from it. Words are mirrors. The real thing wears no words at all.Rule 6 — Halt is not stop.This is the Shiva rule. Failure is not the end. A halt point is the place where the carrier hands the stick to the next runner. The running does not stop — only the carrier changes. The world is a relay race. The energy continues; only the form rests for a moment and resumes through a new shape.3. The Mirror Shape of Odd PrimesA prime is defined the standard way: a number divisible only by 1 and itself. That is the definition. There is no other definition needed for the question “is N prime?”But once you know a number is an odd prime, you can look at its inner shape. Every odd prime has the same shape:N  =  k  +  1  +  khalf  —  mirror  —  halfThe two k’s are equal. The 1 in the middle is the mirror axis. The two halves face each other across the centre.
