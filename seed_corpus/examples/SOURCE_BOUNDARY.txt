## Hard boundary

This is not a confirmed Egyptological translation. It is a tagged thinking-engine report.

- **FACT** = visible image feature or widely supported Egyptology reference.
- **REVIEW** = plausible working interpretation, not proven.
- **SYNTHETIC** = generated meaning, useful fiction, symbol-language expansion.
- **RUMOR/UNVERIFIED** = not used as evidence; only allowed as a pressure-test idea.
- **OPEN** = deliberately unresolved.
