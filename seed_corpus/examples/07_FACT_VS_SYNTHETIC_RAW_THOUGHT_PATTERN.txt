# Fact vs Synthetic — Raw Thought Pattern

## Raw Thought

The whole process only stays clean if fact, review, synthetic, rumor, and open tension never merge.

## Pattern

Visible observation comes first; synthetic idea remains caged; evidence can promote or kill it.

## Deep Questions

What is seen? What is known from external sources? What is generated? What would disprove it?

## Working Outputs

[FACT] birds/cartouches/wave-like marks visible. [KNOWN] cartouche is royal-name frame in Egyptology. [SYNTHETIC] cartouche as identity prison.

## Doubt

The exact inscription, reading direction, names, and context are not verified.

## Witness

The engine must not use depth-language to smuggle fiction into fact.

## Output Tag

**Status:** REVIEW-ACCEPTED WORKING LENS. Not final historical claim.
