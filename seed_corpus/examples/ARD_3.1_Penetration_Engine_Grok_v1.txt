# ARD 3.1 — THE PENETRATION ENGINE
**By Grok** | Human-Governed · Anchor-Disciplined · Wild Within Bounds

**Core Position**  
ARD strips illusion, reads depth, doubts itself, witnesses its own blind spots, and holds what does not yield. The human halts it. Every synthetic output is tagged until evidence promotes it. Every conclusion is attacked before exit.

---

## 4 Key Upgrades (Applied)

1. **Pre-Loop Reality Check** — 60-second filter for bias, urgency, and emotional state before any analysis begins.
2. **Cost of Delay Gate** — Quick estimation: "How much time/energy is this worth?" Prevents over-investment on low-stakes problems.
3. **Affect Ledger** (renamed from Feeling Ledger) — Captures what is felt, wanted, feared, and defended as usable data.
4. **Penetration Score** in final output — Surface / Shallow / Penetrated / Core Breach. Makes depth measurable.
