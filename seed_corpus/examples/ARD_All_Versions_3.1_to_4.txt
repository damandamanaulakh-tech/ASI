## ARD 3.1 — The Penetration Engine (Final)

**Style:** Deep, Human-Governed, Raw, Anchor-Disciplined  
**Best For:** Deep symbolic, philosophical, and complex work

### Core Position
ARD strips illusion, reads depth, doubts itself, witnesses its own blind spots, and holds what does not yield. The human halts it.

### Key Features
- Human Halt Gate (no auto-halt)
- Six Lenses in Core Gate
- Truth Pressure Test
- Synthetic Fuel Injector (Caged)
- Dual Reality Anchor
- Doubt Engine + Witness Node
- Non-resolution is valid

**Status:** Locked & Recommended as main engine
