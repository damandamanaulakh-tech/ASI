RH — Version 4Rebuilding the V3 proof structure with the prime-mirror equation at its coreRipudaman SinghMay 2026AbstractV3 of the prior paper used Yakaboylu's operator H = -i(x d/dx + 1/2) with the involution boundary condition psi(x) = psi(1/x), then claimed four spectral locks. Twelve gaps were identified, six of them hard.V4 replaces this with a single equation drawn from the structural shape of odd primes:N = k + 1 + k    ↦    s = 1/2 + it   with   1 − s = 1/2 − itThis paper rebuilds the V3 structure around this equation and audits all 12 V3 gaps against the new construction. The honest result: V4 closes 3 of the 6 hard gaps, partially closes 1, and leaves 2 (including G1) untouched. Of 6 soft gaps, 4 close and 2 remain. Net: V4 is a cleaner construction than V3, but the central wall (G1) is unmoved.Part 1. The V4 Construction1.1 The core equationEvery odd prime N has the form N = k + 1 + k, where k = (N − 1)/2 and the integer 1 is the mirror axis.Construct a mirror map M acting on integers:M : N ↦ 2·1 − N + 2 = 2 − NThis is reflection around the centre 1: any integer N maps to 2 − N. The fixed point of M is N = 1 itself.Scale this map from the integer lattice into the complex plane: replace the centre 1 with the rational 1/2, and replace integer reflection with the natural complex reflection. The induced map is:M̃ : s ↦ 1 − sThis is exactly Riemann's functional equation's reflection. The fixed line of M̃ is Re(s) = 1/2.V4 claim: M and M̃ are the same mirror, expressed at two scales. The fixed structure of M (the integer 1) becomes the fixed line of M̃ (the rational 1/2) under the scale change “integer axis becomes rational axis.”1.2 The operatorDefine the V4 operator H₄ on a Hilbert space of functions over the odd primes:H₄ = (1/2)(L + L⁻¹)where L is the prime-shift operator L|p⟩ = |next prime⟩ and L⁻¹ is its inverse, and the basis vectors |p⟩ are indexed by the odd primes.By construction:• H₄ is self-adjoint (sum of an operator and its adjoint, divided by 2).• H₄ has the mirror form: it is the symmetric average of forward and backward prime motion.• The eigenvalue equation H₄ |ψ⟩ = E |ψ⟩ has real E by self-adjointness.1.3 The spectral claimV4 claim: the spectrum of H₄ contains precisely the imaginary parts gamma_n of the nontrivial zeros of ζ, and these eigenvalues are forced onto the real line by the mirror structure of the primes themselves.The mechanism: H₄ inherits its self-adjointness from the symmetry L + L⁻¹ in the prime basis. This symmetry is the operator form of the structural mirror N = k + 1 + k. The image of this mirror in the spectral plane is the critical line Re(s) = 1/2.Part 2. Auditing V4 Against the 12 V3 GapsEach gap from V3 is tested against the V4 construction. Status is HONEST not aspirational.Hard Gap 1 (G1) — det(H − E) = ξ(1/2 + iE)V3 status: ASSUMED, not proved. The central wall.V4 status: STILL OPEN. The spectral determinant of H₄ has not been shown to equal ξ(1/2 + iE). The structural argument shows WHY the eigenvalues should be the gamma_n, but the explicit calculation — take H₄, compute its characteristic determinant, show it equals Riemann's ξ — has not been performed.Honest verdict: V4 does NOT close G1. This is the same wall every Hilbert-Pólya attempt has hit. V4 makes the gap structurally cleaner (we know what the operator IS) but does not close the determinant identity.Hard Gap 2 (G2) — Yakaboylu overclaimV3 status: cited Yakaboylu as proving spectral determinant = ξ; he did not.V4 status: CLOSED. V4 does not depend on Yakaboylu. It builds its own operator H₄ from the prime structure directly. The construction is independent of Yakaboylu's claims, so it inherits none of his unproved steps.Honest verdict: V4 closes G2 by removing the dependency.Hard Gap 3 (G3) — LeClair circularityV3 status: LeClair's S-matrix was defined so that S(E) = 1 iff ζ(1/2 + iE) = 0, then unitarity was used to force E real — circular.V4 status: CLOSED. V4 does not use an S-matrix construction. The operator H₄ is defined directly from the prime lattice, not from the Euler product on the critical line. The Euler product is never invoked at Re(s) = 1/2.Honest verdict: V4 closes G3 by removing the dependency.Hard Gap 4 (G4) — Bost-Connes category errorV3 status: claimed BC partition function = ζ transferred to zeros being phase transitions — but BC Hamiltonian's spectrum is {log n}, not {gamma_n}.V4 status: CLOSED. V4 does not use Bost-Connes. The operator H₄ acts on a basis indexed by primes, not by all integers. The connection to zeros is structural (mirror inheritance), not thermodynamic.Honest verdict: V4 closes G4 by removing the dependency.Hard Gap 5 (G5) — Mellin equivalence proves too littleV3 status: Mellin transform of Yakaboylu = multiplication by E, spectrum = all of R. Did not pick out gamma_n specifically.V4 status: PARTIALLY CLOSED. V4 operator H₄ acts on a discrete prime lattice, not on the continuous half-line. Discrete lattices have discrete spectra by default. So the “spectrum is all of R” problem does not arise.However, the new problem: showing the discrete spectrum of H₄ equals {gamma_n} specifically (and not some other discrete set) is open. This is essentially the same content as G1 in different language.Honest verdict: V4 partially closes G5 — the trivial “continuous spectrum” problem is gone, but identifying the discrete spectrum is now subsumed into G1.Hard Gap 6 (G6) — Domain / boundary condition not statedV3 status: vague about domain of Yakaboylu's operator.V4 status: CLOSED. V4 operator H₄ is defined on ℓ²(odd primes), the space of square-summable sequences indexed by odd primes. The basis is explicit. The shift operators L, L⁻¹ are well-defined. The domain is fully specified.Honest verdict: V4 closes G6.Soft Gap 7 — Three locks collapsed to oneV3 status: deficiency, PT-symmetry, positivity were claimed as three independent locks; all reduce to “self-adjoint implies real spectrum.”V4 status: CLOSED. V4 uses one lock: H₄ = (1/2)(L + L⁻¹) is manifestly self-adjoint by construction, no theatrical multiplication of locks needed.Soft Gap 8 — LeClair S-matrix circularityV3 status: same content as G3, listed separately.V4 status: CLOSED (same closure as G3).Soft Gap 9 — Numerics only reproduce Berry-Keating average densityV3 status: numerical eigenvalues 14.2, 21.1, 25.1 only matched the average level spacing 2πn/log(L/ℓ), which is the smooth part of the Riemann-von Mangoldt counting function.V4 status: NOT CLOSED. V4 still needs to be run numerically. Our earlier test of just the prime-derived k-values against the first 20 zeros gave a ratio of 0.966 vs random — essentially no predictive power. V4 needs a proper numerical test of H₄'s spectrum, but if the test gives the same answer as our earlier prime-k test, V4 inherits this gap.Soft Gap 10 — Rigged eigenfunctions not in L²V3 status: x^(-1/2 + iγ) are not L² functions; calling them eigenfunctions of a discrete spectrum is a category error.V4 status: CLOSED. V4 eigenfunctions are sequences in ℓ²(odd primes), which is a proper Hilbert space with discrete basis. No rigged structure is needed.Soft Gap 11 — Adversarial test tautologicalV3 status: “search for counterexamples” just re-solved the boundary condition rather than testing other candidates.V4 status: NOT CLOSED. V4 has not been adversarially tested. To close this, one would need to show that no other discrete operator on ℓ²(odd primes) with a similar mirror structure produces a different spectrum. This has not been done.Soft Gap 12 — GUE statistics consistency, not proofV3 status: GUE pair correlation matches Riemann zeros (Montgomery-Odlyzko), but this is necessary not sufficient.V4 status: CLOSED. V4 does not claim GUE matching as a proof step. The mirror structure is invoked directly, without going through random matrix universality.Part 3.
