What this workbook is	A higher-level comparison + developer discussion pack for a private AI assistant governed by your own ideology and mindset with different response-generation logic.
Share first	Share the Idea Exchange Brief / Stage 0 summary only. Do not share detailed rule templates, scoring logic, or full answer examples in first call.
Share after interest	Share the Developer Fill Form + this workbook sheets: Stage_1_12_Filled, Phase_1_5_Filled, Developer_Scorecard, Dev_Questions.
What developers must prove	They understand RAG/file search, source scoring, intent classification, cost control, privacy, structured outputs, testing/evaluation, and ownership terms.
What not to claim	Do not claim you are training a new ChatGPT from zero. Position it as a private AI reasoning system using existing base models plus custom algorithm/rules/data sources.
Important data note	You can use public/open data and your own data. You cannot directly take hidden training data from existing AI systems.
