THE LOOP SEQUENCE (Step‑by‑Step)### Step 1 – POINT ZERO- Take raw user input (question, claim, theory, mystery, invention seed).- Store **exactly** as typed.### Step 2 – RAW SOURCE CAPTURE- Preserve user’s original words without modification.- This is the reference for all future reality checks.### Step 3 – NOISE STRIPPERSplit input into **six categories**:| Category | Meaning | Example ||----------|---------|---------|| Fact | Verifiable statement | “Sales dropped 20%” || Feeling | Emotional expression | “I feel ignored” || Assumption | Unproven belief | “He must be angry” || Pressure | External constraint | “Deadline is tomorrow” || Claim | Assertion needing proof | “This method works” || Mystery | Unknown or open question | “Why do they resist?” || Invention Seed | New idea or hypothesis | “What if we reverse the process?” |### Step 3.5 – HOLY BOOK / CANONICAL EXTRACTORFor each configured source (Bible, Quran, Gita, etc.):- Search for **all** passages related to Point Zero (keywords + semantic matching – use real API or simulated).- Collect new passages that were **not** retrieved in previous loops.- Stop when **no new passage** appears after 2 consecutive loops → **saturation**.- Output: List of passages + source metadata (book, chapter, verse).### Step 3.6 – URR (UNIFIED RECURSIVE RESONANCE)Compare passages from different sources:- **Resonance** – Two or more sources agree on a claim → increase confidence (boost by 10–20%).- **Dissonance** – Sources contradict each other → mark claim as `⚠️ CONDITIONAL` and add to gap table.- Output a **resonance score** (0–100%) for each claim.### Step 4 – CLAIM EXTRACTIONBreak **Point Zero + all holy book passages** into **atomic claims**:- One claim = one testable unit.- Also list **unknowns** – things the engine cannot determine.- Example: “Boss avoids me + boss is angry” → two claims: (1) boss avoids me (fact? maybe), (2) boss is angry (inference/guess).### Step 5 – DUAL ANALYSIS**A. Exhaustive Analysis** – Run up to 5 internal sub‑loops:- Sub‑loop 1: Gather all possible interpretations- Sub‑loop 2: Find supporting evidence- Sub‑loop 3: Find opposing evidence- Sub‑loop 4: Check for hidden assumptions- Sub‑loop 5: Synthesize best candidate**B. Simplicity Filter** – Check two things:- **Occam’s Razor** – Is there a simpler explanation than the current one?- **Key‑in‑Hand** – Is the answer already obvious? If yes, halt immediately.### Step 6 – REALITY BRIDGE (as you defined)For **every claim**, find at least one **real‑world connection**. Real means:- Proven as of today (scientific consensus, established fact)- Ethically accepted (no torture, fraud, etc.)Connection types:| Type | Example ||------|---------|| Observable event | “The sun rose today” || Known law | “Gravity pulls masses together” || Human behavior | “People avoid pain” || Historical example | “The 2008 financial crisis” || Existing technology | “Smartphones exist” || Measurable proxy | “GDP, temperature, response time” || Scientific constraint | “Nothing travels faster than light” || Economic/social pattern | “Supply and demand” || Verified source | “Peer‑reviewed paper” |If a claim **cannot** be connected to reality → mark `❌ OPEN` and flag as gap.### Step 7 – HUMAN PATTERN GATE (not mind‑reading, only patterns)Check for **six human patterns**:| Pattern | What it detects ||---------|----------------|| Mask / Payoff | Hidden ego, unstated gain || Wound / Threat | Fear, defense, past hurt || Loyalty / Drive | Secret allegiance, identity || Power / Incentive | Who benefits, who controls || Identity / Protection | Self‑image, role preservation |Do **not** invent motives – only flag **if the text or evidence suggests** these patterns.### Step 8 – EVIDENCE LEDGERLabel **each claim** with one of these tags:| Tag | Meaning ||-----|---------|| FACT | Direct observation or verified data || INFERENCE | Logical conclusion from facts || GUESS | Low‑confidence speculation || UNKNOWN | Cannot determine with available info || PERSONAL THEORY | User’s own hypothesis, not externally verified || SYNTHETIC | Generated by fuel injector (fiction) || CONTRADICTED | Opposing evidence exists || NEEDS SOURCE | Claim requires citation but none given |### Step 9 – FALSIFIER / BREAK TESTFor every claim, answer these questions:- What would prove this wrong?- What contradicts it?- What is missing?- What is being forced (assumed without reason)?- Where can this fail?If no good answer → mark `⚠️ CONDITIONAL` or `❌ OPEN`.### Step 10 – PROOF LADDER (auto‑assign by engine rules)Use the **Proof Ladder Rules** provided earlier (Levels 0–7). Assign the highest level that matches the claim’s support.### Step 10.5 – PRE‑CLAIM AUDITOR (Mandatory for 🔒 PROVEN)Before any claim can be marked `🔒 PROVEN`, run four checks:1. **Spectral‑type compatibility** – Is the claim about discrete or continuous phenomena? Is the domain appropriate?  2. **Domain density & boundary validity** – Is the claim well‑defined in its domain (math, physics, human behavior, etc.)?  3. **Circularity test** – Does the conclusion appear in the premises? (Circular → fail)  4. **Distributional validity** – If the claim involves a delta function or distribution, check E≠E′ (pole alone is not a delta).  If **all four green** → `🔒 PROVEN`. If any fail → downgrade to `📖 REFERENCED` or `⚠️ CONDITIONAL`.### Step 11 – RESOLUTION GATEWAYCheck if the problem is:- **Solved** – All claims resolved?  - **Useful** – Does the answer help?  - **Testable** – Can we verify it?  - **Actionable** – Leads to a next move?  - **Reality‑connected** – Passed Reality Bridge?  - **Risk‑controlled** – Major risks identified and manageable?If **all YES** → go to Step 12 (Output Generator).  If **any NO** → go to Step 13 (Critical Logic Wall).### Step 12 – OUTPUT GENERATORShow the following in clear format:```ASKED: [original Point Zero]DELIVERED:- Core Problem: ...- Evidence Ledger: [list of tags]- Human Patterns: [detected]- Reality Bridge: [connections]- Proof Ladder: [level] – [meaning]- Confidence: XX%- Risk: ...- Next Move: ...- Public‑safe output: ... Debt must be cleared for claim to reach `🔒 PROVEN`.### Step 17 – REALITY RE‑ANCHORAfter any synthetic step, check:- Does this still match **Point Zero**?- Does it connect to **reality** (Step 6)?- Does it fit the **Proof Ladder** level?- Is it consistent with **Evidence Ledger**?- Does it serve the **original intent**?If ANY fails → discard or modify.### Step 18 – RETURN + FEED FORWARD- If proof level **improved** → return to Point Zero with Product, start next loop.- If proof level **did not improve** → return to Point Zero but also flag possible stagnation.  - Loop re‑opens.
